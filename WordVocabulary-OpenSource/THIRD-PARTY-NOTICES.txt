Embedded lexical data: ECDICT, via WordCraft and CetVocab.
Full dictionary: https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT/blob/master/ecdict.csv
Official source Git blob: c4ade63ea08cf39d9c3475e96929036d64d94c94
770611 source rows; 767533 normalized entries with Chinese translations.
Full-source data is stored in data/full; the earlier subset remains embedded as a fallback.
Only word, phonetic and Chinese meanings are included; no examples.
https://github.com/chen-dongchai/WordCraft--/blob/main/WordCraft/Data/wordbank.db
https://github.com/ChihoYiu/CetVocab/tree/main/app/src/main/assets/wordpacks
